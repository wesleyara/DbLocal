# Keyboard shortcuts

Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere outside a field to open the cheat sheet, or <kbd>F1</kbd> to open this help. <kbd>⌘</kbd> is <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> outside macOS.

Shortcuts do not fire while you type in a field or in the SQL editor, except the global ones that make sense there: the command palette, save, run and search.
